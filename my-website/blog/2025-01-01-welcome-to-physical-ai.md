# Welcome to the Future of Embodied Intelligence

We're excited to launch this comprehensive textbook on **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics**. This is more than just another robotics resource—it's an AI-native learning platform designed for builders who want to create intelligent systems that move, sense, and interact with the physical world.

## Why Physical AI Matters Now

For decades, artificial intelligence lived exclusively in the digital realm. But a profound shift is underway. AI is breaking free from screens and entering the physical world through robots, autonomous vehicles, and humanoid systems.

<!--truncate-->

Three converging trends have made Physical AI a reality:

1. **AI Model Breakthroughs**: Foundation models (GPT, CLIP, diffusion models) now enable robots to understand language, recognize objects, and reason about tasks at unprecedented levels.

2. **Simulation Advances**: Tools like NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Gazebo allow robots to train millions of times faster than real-world interaction.

3. **Hardware Improvements**: Cheaper sensors, more powerful edge compute, and better actuators make advanced robots economically viable.

## What Makes This Textbook Different
